INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Ref: INT-2026-041
Originator: District Special Branch, Desk 2
Date: 2026-03-23
Classification: RESTRICTED - internal circulation
Subject: Layered fund movement associated with the Nandanvan investment complaints
---
Following the complaints registered at Nandanvan and Vashisht Market police stations, a preliminary look at the account activity of the concern named in those complaints suggests a layering arrangement rather than a simple diversion of funds. Sums collected from complainants appear to be aggregated in a single current account and then moved outward in large single tranches to individual accounts that have little or no prior transaction history. Two of the recipient accounts are understood to have been opened in January 2026 with minimal KYC. In at least one instance the same sum, less a small deduction, is believed to have moved through three accounts inside two days before being drawn in cash. The proprietor is suspected to be operating a second entity registered at a residential address in Tilakwadi, though the relationship between the two concerns is unconfirmed at this stage. The identity of the person controlling the cash at the final stage has not been established. Bank statements have been called for and this desk will revise the assessment on receipt.


INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Ref: INT-2026-047
Originator: District Special Branch, Desk 4
Date: 2026-03-26
Classification: RESTRICTED - internal circulation
Subject: Depot and cold storage activity, Ghatpimpri and Nandanvan
---
Reporting from two independent sources indicates that consignments of unaccounted goods are being consolidated at a cold storage facility in the Nandanvan industrial belt and moved out by road on nights selected in advance. Source A, whose reliability is assessed as generally reliable, states that the movement dates are communicated only two days ahead and only to the drivers involved. Source B, untested, suggests that a broker rather than the depot operator decides the timing, and that this broker also handles work for parties in Nandanvan West unconnected with the depot. The seizure at Rewa Gate on 20/03/2026 is consistent with source A's account. It is not presently possible to say whether the goods originate from a single supplier. A loader from the workers colony is suspected to be acting as a lookout on loading nights; this is based on a single source and should not be treated as established. Toll records and gate registers have been requisitioned.


INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Ref: INT-2026-052
Originator: District Special Branch, Desk 2
Date: 2026-04-01
Classification: RESTRICTED - internal circulation
Subject: Possible linkage between the Nandanvan financial matter and the depot matter
---
Attention is invited to a point of overlap between two files at present held separately. A freight broker operating from a residence in Tilakwadi appears in the depot reporting as the person setting movement dates, and separately appears to have received two payments described as an advisory retainer from an entity associated with the proprietor named in the investment complaints. If confirmed, this would be the only established point of contact between the two groups; no other individual has so far been reported as common to both. It is emphasised that a retainer payment is not by itself evidence of a criminal arrangement and the broker may have a legitimate consultancy engagement. Nevertheless the desk assesses, with low to moderate confidence, that this individual is the most probable channel through which proceeds from the first matter are being placed into the logistics operation. Call data for the numbers attributed to him has been sought.


INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Ref: INT-2026-055
Originator: District Special Branch, Desk 1
Date: 2026-04-05
Classification: RESTRICTED - internal circulation
Subject: Items examined and set aside
---
Three matters flagged during routine screening have been examined and are not assessed as of interest. First, a series of high value cash deposits in a savings account at Ganjbasoda was queried; the account holder has produced a registered sale deed for agricultural land and the deposits correspond in amount and date to the instalments recorded therein. Second, a subscriber whose call pattern shows heavy activity between 01:00 and 04:00 hrs daily was examined; the subscriber is employed on night shift at a business process outsourcing unit and the called parties are a rotating group of colleagues rather than any fixed contact. Third, a residential address in Nandanvan appearing in two separate files was checked and found to be a shared tenancy occupied by unrelated tenants, one of whom is employed in the software sector and has no other point of contact with the persons of interest. Desks are advised to record these three items as examined and cleared so that they are not raised again in subsequent reporting.
