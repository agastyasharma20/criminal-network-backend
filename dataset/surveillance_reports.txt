SURVEILLANCE REPORT
Report No: SR-1002
Date: 2026-03-17
Officer: HC S. Ambatkar, Ghatpimpri Police Station
Subject(s): Kaveri Depot Yard, Ghatpimpri Road; unidentified drivers
---
Static observation of the Kaveri Depot Yard gate was maintained between 18:00 hrs on 17/03/2026 and 02:00 hrs on 18/03/2026 from the embankment adjoining the approach road. Yard activity during daylight hours appeared routine. From about 20:30 hrs the yard lights on the northern side were switched off while loading continued under two portable lamps. A white truck reversed into bay two at 21:10 hrs. The driver, a man of about thirty five years, made and received calls almost continuously during the loading and was heard raising his voice on at least three occasions. A second man arrived at 22:15 hrs on a black motorcycle, spoke to the driver briefly, and thereafter remained near the gate for the rest of the period, stopping one auto rickshaw that attempted to turn into the approach road. No loading manifest was seen being handed over. Loading concluded at about 23:50 hrs but the truck did not depart, remaining inside the yard overnight. Registration numbers could not be read from the observation point owing to the position of the vehicle and the absence of lighting. Report submitted for information and further tasking.


SURVEILLANCE REPORT
Report No: SR-1004
Date: 2026-03-19
Officer: PSI M. Talekar, Nandanvan Police Station
Subject(s): Nandanvan Cold Storage, Plot 7, Nandanvan Industrial Belt
---
Observation was mounted at the Nandanvan Cold Storage from 19:30 hrs on 19/03/2026 following information received. At 21:03 hrs a grey Maruti Ertiga entered the industrial belt junction and proceeded towards the storage gate, arriving at 21:11 hrs. Two men alighted. The first was identified from photographs on record as Bhargav Ronghe of Ronghe Estate. The second was a man of about forty three years in a dark half sleeve shirt whom the informant identified as a freight broker known in the area as Talpade. Both proceeded to the loading bay on the eastern side. Between 21:15 and 21:35 hrs the two men were observed in conversation with a woman in a maroon salwar suit who had come out of the office cabin carrying a register; she was addressed by the taller man as Ruksana. At 21:22 hrs the woman returned to the cabin and the two men remained at the bay where a white truck was being loaded. A young man on a black motorcycle was stationed at the outer gate throughout and questioned this officer's informant when he passed on foot at 21:29 hrs. At 21:44 hrs the truck departed towards the bypass. The Ertiga left at 21:51 hrs. Photography was not possible owing to the absence of cover on the approach.


SURVEILLANCE REPORT
Report No: SR-1005
Date: 2026-03-22
Officer: ASI R. Kolhatkar, Purana Peth Police Station
Subject(s): Nilesh Wagholikar (alias Nilesh W.); Suhail Barkatpuri
---
Observation was maintained at Wagholikar Wada, Purana Peth between 08:00 and 12:00 hrs. At 09:05 hrs a man arrived by auto rickshaw carrying a cloth bag and was received by the subject at the shop shutter. This person was identified by a local source as one Suhel, said to run a travel agency at Chhota Bazaar. The two remained inside the shop for about twenty minutes with the shutter half drawn. At 09:28 hrs the visitor left without the bag. Subject thereafter made a telephone call lasting approximately four minutes while standing on the road. At 10:40 hrs a white Hyundai i20 arrived and was parked opposite the shop; the driver did not alight. Subject entered the vehicle briefly at 11:15 hrs, remained inside for six minutes, and returned to the shop. The vehicle left at 11:55 hrs towards the Purana Peth crossing. It is submitted that the pattern of visits at this shop does not correspond to the stated business.


SURVEILLANCE REPORT
Report No: SR-1007
Date: 2026-03-28
Officer: HC S. Ambatkar, Ghatpimpri Police Station
Subject(s): Kaveri Depot Yard; white truck and blue truck
---
Further to SR-1002, observation was resumed on the night of 28/03/2026 from a position on the eastern embankment affording a view of the yard gate. A blue truck entered the yard at 20:55 hrs and a white truck at 21:19 hrs. Loading commenced at approximately 21:40 hrs with the northern lights again switched off. On this occasion the registration of the blue vehicle was read as MP09QR2208 using a night vision monocular. A man believed to be the yard operator was present and was observed to hand a bundle of currency notes to each of two drivers before departure. At 22:04 hrs a woman arrived by auto rickshaw, entered the office cabin, and left at 22:31 hrs. The white truck departed at 23:12 hrs towards the toll plaza. It is noted that the sequence of events closely resembles that observed on 19/03/2026 at the Nandanvan Cold Storage, with the same interval between arrival of the loading party and departure of the loaded vehicle. Recommend that toll plaza records for lanes two and five be obtained for the nights of 19/03, 28/03 and any subsequent activity.


SURVEILLANCE REPORT
Report No: SR-1009
Date: 2026-04-04
Officer: PSI M. Talekar, Nandanvan Police Station
Subject(s): Shrivardhan Apartments, Nandanvan - verification of residents
---
Verification was carried out at Shrivardhan Apartments, Nandanvan, in connection with the registered address of a commercial vehicle. Enquiry with the society secretary establishes that Flat 3B is a three room tenement let out on shared tenancy to three unrelated working tenants, of whom one is employed as a driver with a depot at Ghatpimpri and another is employed with a software support firm and keeps regular office hours. The society register shows two vehicles standing in the name of Flat 3B occupants, a red motorcycle and a grey scooter. The commercial vehicle under enquiry has never been parked at the premises according to the watchman, who has been on duty at this building for four years. The software support tenant was interviewed and stated that he moved in during May 2025, has no acquaintance with the depot staff beyond sharing the tenancy, and leaves for work at about 09:15 hrs daily. His employer's identity card and appointment letter were seen and appear regular. It is submitted that the coincidence of address in this case appears to be a consequence of shared tenancy and not of association.
